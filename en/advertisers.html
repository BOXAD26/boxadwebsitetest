Questions and answers.

How do you measure results?

Every campaign has a dedicated QR code. We agree a goal with you before starting (for example bookings or enquiries), count scans and enquiries until the boxes run out, then send you a report with boxes used, scans, gift codes claimed and used, and new customers. We ask everyone who responds how they heard about you. Scans are aggregated and anonymous, and we never add invented figures.

What counts as success?

We decide together before starting: a number of bookings, visits or enquiries. We don’t promise sales: we measure the response with QR codes.

How does the proposal work?

You tell us what you want to achieve and we reply within a day with a proposal for the whole campaign: area, number of boxes, start date and the result we measure together. The number of boxes depends on the goal and the area. The boxes are paid to the printer before printing; our fee only when the boxes have run out. Distribution lasts until the boxes are used up.

How long does it take to start?

From artwork approval to delivery to pizzerias takes a few weeks. The quote gives you the exact date.

Who prepares the artwork?

We prepare the artwork with you, starting from your logo and the offer you want to communicate. Your brand is the hero, in full colour, with one clear message and a gift behind the QR: no shouting, because the box arrives at a pleasant moment. You approve before printing.

Are the boxes food-safe?

We print only on the outside, with water-based inks suitable for food use.

Doubts we often hear.

“This way the box is no longer my pizzeria’s box.”

During the campaign the box carries the advertiser’s artwork: that’s exactly what makes it free. The campaign is temporary, the pizza stays yours, and the rest of the time you use your usual boxes.

“Customers will think we advertise for others.”

It can work the other way: like a sponsor on a team’s shirt, it signals a link with local businesses.

“A colourful box looks cheap.”

Only badly made artwork looks cheap. A well-designed box can look more polished than a plain one.

“What if the boxes don’t arrive on time?”

You’re never left without: we plan deliveries with a buffer, and your own boxes stay available as backup. Campaign boxes replace them only once they’ve arrived.

“How do we stay in touch?”

However you prefer: WhatsApp or email. We care about a direct, simple relationship.

Privacy

Privacy notice.

Information pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Data controller

BOXAD., sole proprietorship (ditta individuale) of Abdulmalik Bin Yasad, Viale Gian Giacomo Felissent 4A, Treviso, VAT no. (P. IVA) 04870820273. Contact: info@boxad.it · PEC malekz27@pec.it.

What data we process

Only the data you enter in the site’s forms (contact request, quote, sample box, pizzeria applications): name, company or pizzeria, email, phone or mobile, postcode and area, message and, for pizzerias, information about box production. For proposal requests, also the sector, area and goal you indicate.

Why and on what basis

We use the data to reply to your request, prepare a quote or assess your application (Art. 6.1.b GDPR, pre-contractual measures requested by you). If a relationship starts, we also keep it for tax and accounting legal obligations (Art. 6.1.c). We do not profile, we do not sell data and we do not use it for marketing without your separate consent.

How the forms work

When you submit a form, the data passes through the Formspree service (Formspree, Inc., United States), which forwards it to our info@boxad.it mailbox and keeps it in its own archive for up to 30 days. Formspree processes the data on our behalf. The transfer to the United States takes place with the safeguards required by the GDPR. The site itself does not store form data.

Retention

For as long as needed to handle the request. If it does not lead to a contract, no more than 12 months. If a contract is signed, for the periods required by law (normally 10 years for accounting records).

Recipients

The data is seen only by the controller and by any technical providers who process it on our behalf (for example email and hosting), bound by a data processing agreement. We do not transfer it outside the European Economic Area except with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

Site statistics

We count visits to the pages of boxad.it and form submissions with GoatCounter, a cookie-free statistics service that does not keep your IP address and does not recognise you: we only see aggregate numbers, for example “300 visits to the Advertisers page, most from Google”. To count, your browser sends GoatCounter the page address, the referring site and the screen size. GoatCounter does not receive what you write in the forms. Legal basis: legitimate interest in understanding how many people use the site and where they come from (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Campaign QR codes

The QR code on the boxes opens a page on boxad.it (addresses starting with boxad.it/r/). On these pages we count visits and button taps with GoatCounter, a cookie-free statistics service that does not keep your IP address and does not recognise you: we only see aggregate numbers, for example “120 visits, 9 WhatsApp taps”. To count, your browser sends GoatCounter the page address, the referring site and the screen size. Legal basis: legitimate interest in measuring campaign results (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

If you send a request from a QR page, the data (name, phone, email, message) goes through Formspree like the other forms, reaches BOXAD. and is forwarded to the advertiser named on the page, who uses it to contact you as an independent controller. BOXAD. uses it only to count the requests the campaign produced and keeps it for no more than 12 months. If you tap WhatsApp or Call, you contact the advertiser directly: BOXAD. only counts the tap and does not see the message or the call.

On some QR pages you can pick a gift and receive a personal code to show at the shop. In that case we record only the code, the campaign, the gift you picked, when you said you would come in, the pizzeria the box came from (or whether a friend sent you the link) and the dates the code was requested and used. When the shop uses the code, it may note whether you bought something, in which spending band (for example under or over 200 euros) and whether you were already its customer. We do not ask for your name, phone or email. This data is kept in a BOXAD. Google sheet (Google processes it on our behalf), is used only to count campaign results and is deleted within 24 months. Your browser remembers the code you received (local storage, not a tracking cookie), so if you scan again you get the same code. If you send the code to the shop on WhatsApp, the shop sees your name and number as an independent controller: BOXAD. does not receive them. Legal basis: legitimate interest in measuring campaign results (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Reviews

If you leave a review from the page boxad.it/en/review/ we record your full name, business, ratings, text, suggestions and your choice about publication, with the date and the campaign. This data is kept in the same BOXAD. Google sheet (Google processes it on our behalf). We use it to improve the service (legitimate interest, Art. 6.1.f GDPR). We publish the review on the site and on our social pages only if you allow it, with your name or only your initials as you chose (consent, Art. 6.1.a GDPR), and we do not edit it. Suggestions on what to improve are never published. You can withdraw permission at any time by writing to info@boxad.it: we remove it from the site within 7 days. Unpublished reviews are deleted within 24 months.

Cookies and typefaces

The site does not use cookies. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, without cookies, as explained above. Apart from this count, the site does not load typefaces, scripts or other resources from third-party services.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing (Arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to info@boxad.it. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).
